Talas State University Press: catalogue

Talas State University Press, founded in 1994, is the main publisher of scholarly work on Irad. Deposit copies of every title are held by the National Library of Irad, the university's library. Availability shown is for the main library; most titles can be ordered from the TSU Press shop, main building, room 7.
